# Malus Support

Malus converts the useful content of the active webpage into a local Markdown file.

## Getting started

1. Open a fully loaded `http://` or `https://` webpage.
2. Select the Malus toolbar icon.
3. Select **Save as Markdown**.
4. Open the downloaded `.md` file in your preferred editor or notes application.

## If Malus cannot read a page

- Refresh the page and wait for its content to load before trying again.
- Open the individual article or job-detail page rather than a search-results view.
- Expand content such as job descriptions when the site uses an unfamiliar control.
- Confirm that the page is ordinary rendered HTML. Browser settings pages, protected frames, canvas-only applications, embedded PDFs, media, inaccessible paywalls, and content absent from the page cannot be converted reliably.

## Privacy

Conversion happens locally. Malus does not upload, retain, sell, or share the active page or generated Markdown file. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

## Report a problem

Include the public page URL, what you expected, what happened, and the Malus version. Do not include private page content, credentials, application-form answers, or other sensitive information.
